# Academy: Berater refusals and how to talk about them (November 2026)

Date: 14 November 2026. Status: desk research and proposed wording. This is not legal advice. For a live case, the participant should rely on their Agentur für Arbeit, on [Merkblatt 6](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/merkblatt-6-weiterbildung_ba015381.pdf), and if needed on independent advice.

Triggered by a discovery interview with a test engineer in Berlin on 14 November 2026: [interviews](interviews.md). Builds on [laid-off developers, Agentur and reachability](laid-off-developers-and-agentur.md), [market sizing](market-sizing.md), [the 3-month design](three-month-course-design.md) and [the AZAV ops plan](azav-certification.md).

Primary sources only for legal rules. Community stories and vendor guides are labelled as such and are not treated as law.

## 1. The conversion risk

The test engineer we interviewed has about 10 years of experience. At 4 or 5 appointments her previous Berater refused a Bildungsgutschein, saying she does not need training: big experience, no need to study. She also heard "in our Agentur we don't give Gutscheine", a claim that she needed 5 years of work in Germany (she already had that), and then that she did not pay enough taxes. Her new Berater wants German first, then Weiterbildung. In her experience, vouchers are rarely given at the Arbeitsagentur. She suggested trying the Jobcenter as well.

People she knows who did get a voucher were unhappy with the courses (huge classes, nobody checks the learning, people sit out the hours). That is a quality problem, not a substitute for an honest warning about approval.

This matches earlier desk research. The legal test is whether work is realistic without the course. A senior who looks easy to place is often refused. Chances improve when the person brings regional job ads that ask for a skill they do not have, plus a list of unsuccessful applications ([Agentur research §3](laid-off-developers-and-agentur.md); the working example is a public post on r/arbeitsleben, October 2025). The 3-month design already flagged "You are employable" as a real refusal for seniors, and proposed a Vermittler one-pager.

The main conversion risk for this audience is therefore the Berater refusing because of experience ("Sie sind qualifiziert genug"), plus local inconsistency and incorrect rules. Tell people this before they count on a voucher. Do not promise that a better argument will get the voucher approved.

## 2. What the law and the BA actually say

### 2.1 Notwendigkeit and discretion

For an Anpassungsqualifizierung there is no automatic legal entitlement. [§ 81 Abs. 1 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__81.html) lets the Agentur fund training if all of the following are true:

1. the training is necessary to integrate the person into work while they are unemployed, or to prevent looming unemployment,
2. the Agentur has counselled them before the course starts,
3. the measure and the provider are approved for funding (AZAV).

[§ 81 Abs. 1a SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__81.html) also recognises necessity for unemployed people when "durch den Erwerb erweiterter beruflicher Kompetenzen die individuelle Beschäftigungsfähigkeit verbessert wird und sie nach Lage und Entwicklung des Arbeitsmarktes zweckmäßig ist." This is the statutory hook for experienced people who already have a profession and need added skills.

The BA's [Fachliche Weisungen FbW, Stand 01.01.2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/dok_ba031590.pdf) (FW to § 81, Ziffer 1) say:

- These payments are discretionary under [§ 39 SGB I](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_1/__39.html). There is no Rechtsanspruch except in the Abschluss cases of § 81 Abs. 2 and 3. If the individual conditions are met, the person has a right to an error-free exercise of discretion ("Anspruch auf ermessensfehlerfreie Entscheidung").
- Notwendigkeit is an unbestimmter Rechtsbegriff, not pure discretion. It "ist zu bejahen, wenn die Förderung der beruflichen Weiterbildung geeignet und erforderlich ist, um den angestrebten Zweck zu erreichen." It "betrifft die Frage, ob ohne die konkret in Betracht gezogene Weiterbildung eine Vermittlung möglich ist."
- Unemployment alone does not create necessity. "Es müssen Qualifikationsdefizite vorliegen, die durch die Teilnahme an der Weiterbildung abgebaut werden und die mit Blick auf die zu erwartenden Beschäftigungsmöglichkeiten zu einer beruflichen Integration in den ersten Arbeitsmarkt führen."
- Once necessity is established, the BA is bound "als nur noch bei Vorliegen wichtiger Gründe abgelehnt werden dürfte." The next step is Auswahlermessen under § 7 SGB III (the most suitable service for lasting integration, including economy and suitability).
- The decision, including the finding on necessity, must be documented in the counselling note.

A local BA page says the same test in plain language: "Ist eine Arbeitsaufnahme auch ohne eine Förderung realistisch, kann kein Bildungsgutschein ausgehändigt werden" ([Agentur Ludwigsburg, FAQs Förderleistungen](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/ludwigsburg/faq-foerderleistungen), checked 14 November 2026). That is an official local formulation of Notwendigkeit, not a special Ludwigsburg rule.

### 2.2 Berlin's 2026 plan for IT

The [Bildungszielplanung Berlin 2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/datei/bildungszielplanung_berlin_2026_ba264851.pdf) is an orientation framework for individual funding decisions, not a promise to fund a named course. For IT- und naturwissenschaftliche Dienstleistungsberufe it says existing digital core skills (for example programming, IT security, cloud, data management) should be deepened "auf Experten-/ und Spezialistenniveau." It also says: "Aktuell ist ein Quereinstieg in der IT-Branche nur schwer möglich, erforderlich ist eine akademische Vorbildung." The introduction names artificial intelligence as a driver of change. The IT section does not name "coding agents". Use this as support for an experienced-level Anpassungsqualifizierung, not as a guarantee.

### 2.3 Written application, Bescheid, Widerspruch

Applications for social benefits are filed with the competent body and can be made without a special form ([§ 16 SGB I](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_1/__16.html)). The Agentur must help people file a clear and complete application. [Merkblatt 6](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/merkblatt-6-weiterbildung_ba015381.pdf) says to apply at the Agentur for the place of residence, to get counselling before the course starts, and that the Agentur notifies the decision in writing. A written Bescheid is also issued "wenn Ihrem Antrag nicht oder nicht in vollem Umfang entsprochen werden kann."

A written or electronic administrative act must state the essential factual and legal reasons. For a discretionary decision, the reasons must also show the points the authority considered when using its discretion ([§ 35 SGB X](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_10/__35.html)).

If the person disagrees with a written Bescheid, they can file a Widerspruch. [Merkblatt 6](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/merkblatt-6-weiterbildung_ba015381.pdf), section 9, and [§ 84 SGG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgg/__84.html): within one month of notification; in writing, in the permitted electronic form, or in person zur Niederschrift, at the Agentur that issued the Bescheid. The Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung on the Bescheid is the text to follow. If the Widerspruch is not granted in full, there is a written Widerspruchsbescheid, which can be taken to the Sozialgericht. An oral "no" in a meeting is not, by itself, a Bescheid. Without a written decision there is nothing to appeal. We do not promise that a Widerspruch will succeed.

The BA's technical instructions also say a provider must not file the application under a power of attorney from the customer, because that would interfere with the customer's legal freedom to choose a provider (FW FbW, FW to § 81, Ziffer 6). We can give the participant a draft letter. We cannot apply for them.

### 2.4 Agentur, Jobcenter and Weiterbildungsberatung

[The BA Bildungsgutschein page](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein) (checked 14 November 2026): funding depends on individual conditions, discussed in a personal counselling appointment. From 1 January 2025, financial support for Jobcenter clients also comes from the Agentur für Arbeit. The voucher is usually limited in time, region and educational goal. The chosen provider and the course must be approved by a fachkundige Stelle. Starting a course while on Arbeitslosengeld usually means the benefit continues.

[BMAS, 19 December 2024](https://www.bmas.de/DE/Service/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2024/das-aendert-sich-im-neuen-jahr.html) and Merkblatt 6 (notes at the start) describe the split: if a Jobcenter sees a need for Weiterbildung, it refers the person to the local Agentur. The Agentur does the Weiterbildungsberatung, checks the conditions, approves and funds the measure. The Jobcenter remains the case owner for Bürgergeld and for integration after the course.

So "try the Jobcenter" is not a second voucher office for someone already on ALG I. It is the first contact if the person receives Bürgergeld, or later moves onto it. Since 2025 the voucher itself is still issued by the Agentur.

Counselling before the course starts is a statutory condition ([§ 81 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__81.html)). The public route is: contact the Agentur or, for Bürgergeld, the Jobcenter, and ask for a counselling appointment ([BA steps](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein)).

A change of Berater is sometimes possible in practice (a further appointment, or a review by a team lead). I have not found a legal right to a new Berater in the sources above, so do not promise one.

### 2.5 Employed people: Qualifizierungschancengesetz / § 82 SGB III

The common name is Qualifizierungschancengesetz. The current legal basis for funding people who already have a job is [§ 82 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__82.html). Among other conditions: the content goes beyond short, workplace-only updates; the course lasts more than 120 hours; the measure and the provider are approved. This is also a discretionary decision. The employer usually shares the course cost, with the share depending on company size; in small firms the law says a cost share should normally be waived. The employer can apply, including for several employees. Official overview: [BA, individuelle Förderung von Beschäftigten](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/unternehmen/finanziell/foerderung-von-weiterbildung/individuelle-foerderung). Details and current shares should be taken from that page and from § 82, not from this memo's summary. This is a real but secondary path for the first cohort ([market sizing §4](market-sizing.md)).

### 2.6 Rules we have not found

I have not found, in § 81, in the FW FbW or in Merkblatt 6:

- a requirement of 5 years of work in Germany for a Bildungsgutschein,
- a "you have not paid enough taxes" test for the voucher (contribution periods matter for Arbeitslosengeld, which is a different decision),
- a lawful blanket policy that "in our Agentur we don't give Gutscheine." Notwendigkeit is an individual assessment. The Berlin Bildungszielplanung is an orientation framework for that assessment.

If a Berater uses one of these lines, the useful response is to ask for the legal basis, file a written application, and request a written Bescheid. Do not tell the participant that the refusal is automatically unlawful. We are not in a position to decide that.

A language exam before Weiterbildung, as in the test engineer interview (B2 first), is what her Berater is doing. It is not a general statutory condition sourced here.

## 4. Participant guide: talking to the Berater

This is a preparation guide, not a script that guarantees approval, and not legal representation.

### 4.1 The arguments that match the legal test

1. Necessity under § 81 Abs. 1 Nr. 1: without this specific course, placement into the jobs now being advertised is not realistic. Show this with current regional job ads that require skills you do not yet have (for this programme: shipping software with coding agents, or building products on top of LLMs), and with rejection letters or a list of unsuccessful applications. The official question is whether Vermittlung is possible without this Weiterbildung (FW FbW; Ludwigsburg FAQ).
2. § 81 Abs. 1a: even if you already have a profession and years of experience, necessity is also recognised when added professional skills improve your employability and that is sensible given the labour market. Experience is not, by itself, the legal test.
3. The Berlin 2026 plan for IT asks for existing digital skills to be deepened at expert and specialist level, and says a career change into IT is currently very hard. That supports an experienced-level Anpassungsqualifizierung, not a beginner Umschulung.
4. This course is an Anpassungsqualifizierung, not a new Berufsabschluss. The gap is the skills now listed in job postings, plus interview practice (take-homes, live coding, how to talk about a project). Do not claim the course will get the person a job.
5. Bring the formal course evidence once it exists: the AZAV certificates, the Maßnahmenummer, the listing on mein NOW (the public successor of KURSNET), and the written offer (Bildungsangebot) with dates, hours, price and educational goal.

If the Berater says "our office does not give vouchers" or cites 5 years in Germany or unpaid taxes, ask for the legal basis and for a written Bescheid. Do not argue as if you were counsel.

### 4.2 What to bring

- 5 to 10 current, preferably regional Stellenanzeigen that require the missing skills, with the date and the source.
- Rejection letters or, if letters were never sent, a dated list of applications and outcomes.
- CV and a short note on the skills gap (what you already ship, what the ads now ask for, why this course closes that gap).
- Once they exist: the Träger and Maßnahme AZAV certificates, the Maßnahmenummer, the mein NOW / KURSNET listing, and the written Bildungsangebot.
- Any previous written correspondence with the Agentur.

Ask for the counselling appointment before the course starts. Do not enrol, and do not leave a job, on the assumption that a voucher will arrive (Merkblatt 6 warns against quitting a job in order to start a course).

### 4.3 If the answer is no

Ask for a written Bescheid. File a written application if the refusal was only oral, so that a decision has to be issued in writing (Merkblatt 6, section 8; § 16 SGB I).

If a Bescheid arrives and you disagree, the Widerspruch period is one month from notification, unless the Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung says otherwise. File it at the issuing Agentur, in writing, in the permitted electronic form, or in person zur Niederschrift. Keep proof of receipt. The detailed reasons can usually follow if the first letter is filed on time and says that reasons will follow, but follow the Belehrung on the Bescheid. We can help gather documents. We do not file the Widerspruch for the participant and we do not promise a result.

### 4.4 Alternatives

- Jobcenter: the first contact if you receive Bürgergeld. It assesses need and refers you to the Agentur, which since 1 January 2025 does the counselling, the decision and the funding. It is not a parallel issuer of the voucher for someone already on ALG I.
- A further counselling appointment, or a review by a team lead. There is no sourced legal right to a new Berater.
- The Agentur's Weiterbildungsberatung (the statutory counselling under § 81 Abs. 1 Nr. 2). Employed people can also ask about [Berufsberatung im Erwerbsleben](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/berufsberatung-im-erwerbsleben).
- If you have a job and your employer will support training: funding under § 82 SGB III (Qualifizierungschancengesetz). The employer usually applies. This is also discretionary.

## 5. What we as the Träger can provide

Once both AZAV certificates exist (do not offer this pack as if the course were already fundable):

1. A one-pager for the Berater, and a template letter the participant can submit in their own name. Content: the legal test in short (§ 81 Abs. 1 and 1a, the Berlin 2026 IT lines), what the course is and is not (Anpassungsqualifizierung for experienced developers, not a beginner Umschulung, no job promise), the Maßnahmenummer, dates, UE, price, teaching language, online or hybrid format, and a short list of job ads. The participant signs and files it. We do not file it under a power of attorney (FW FbW, Ziffer 6).
2. The KURSNET / mein NOW listing, with the tick for funding with a Bildungsgutschein, plus the AZAV certificates and the written Bildungsangebot.
3. A maintained list of current job postings that require the skills the course teaches, preferably Berlin and Germany, with date and source. This is the evidence pack the design doc already proposed, and it matches how people in the earlier research actually obtained a voucher.

We can also walk the participant through Merkblatt 6 and the appointment. We cannot promise approval, extend ALG I, or act as their lawyer.

## 6. Open questions for the AZAV work

1. When will the Trägerzulassung, the Maßnahmezulassung, the Maßnahmenummer and the mein NOW / KURSNET listing exist, so they can go into the Berater pack? Public copy must not say the course is already fundable before both certificates exist.
2. Who drafts and reviews the participant letter and the Berater one-pager (we ourselves or an external adviser)? The pack must stay accurate and must not be filed by us on the participant's behalf.
3. Exact Bildungsangebot fields: Maßnahmenummer, start and end dates, UE, KldB, price, teaching language, online or hybrid, and copies of both certificates.
4. Will Berlin Vermittler accept an English-taught, live online or hybrid 3-month Anpassungsqualifizierung for this audience? The ops plan notes that English teaching is allowed, but the Vermittler still judges job prospects. Any wording needed on region or language?
5. How should operators handle a Berater who postpones Weiterbildung until a language exam (B2 in the test engineer interview)? Is there a formal requirement we should document, or is this local practice?
6. After an oral refusal: how far do we help with a written Antrag, a Bescheid request or a Widerspruch (document pack only, or a referred adviser)? We must not overstep into unauthorised legal practice.
7. Who maintains the job-posting list, how often, and for which region? The same list may be asked for by the fachkundige Stelle and by a Vermittler.
8. Do we want a separate employer pack for § 82 / Qualifizierungschancengesetz (including a Sammelantrag path), or is that later than the first cohort?
9. If the 2027 Bildungszielplanung changes the IT focus, who updates the one-pager?
