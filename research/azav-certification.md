# AZAV Certification Plan (Trägerzulassung and Maßnahmezulassung)

Research from 14 November 2026, based on primary sources (laws, BA, Beirat, ZFU, certifier procedures, Berlin Senate). 

The question: how do we get DataTalksClub Academy certified so a Bildungsgutschein can pay for it? How long does it take, does an April 2027 start work, and which legal entity applies?

Course assumptions come from the [3-month course design](three-month-course-design.md): 520 UE, fully live, English, 3 months, KldB 43414, calculated on 12 seats, with a surcharge of up to +25% over the B-DKS. Delivery can be live online or in person in Berlin. I teach alone; named backup teachers cover absence; mentors review projects. There is no second standing instructor (decided on 14 November 2026). The case for the surcharge rests on documented special costs (AI tools, cloud/GPU, mentors and backup teachers, platform, optional room, career coaching): see the [cost justification](azav-cost-justification.md).

Labels: **[Law]** statute or regulation read in the original. **[BA]** BA, Beirat or a public authority page. **[FKS]** a certifier's own published procedure. **[Sec]** consultant or vendor page (secondary, maybe self-serving). **[Inf]** my inference.

## Summary

- **Two certificates from one private certifier (fachkundige Stelle, FKS):** the Trägerzulassung (the business, 5 years, annual surveillance audit) and the Maßnahmezulassung (this course, normally 3 years). The BA is not involved up to +25% over the B-DKS. **[Law] [BA]**
- **No ISO 9001 needed.** We need an AZAV QM system that covers the 9 points of § 2 (4) AZAV and is actually applied before the audit. **[Law] [FKS]**
- **No ZFU / FernUSG approval if the contract promises live teaching.** BGH, 5 February 2026 (III ZR 137/25): mostly live online teaching is not Fernunterricht. Recordings given to participants count as asynchronous (BGH, 12 June 2025, III ZR 109/24), so **we do not promise recordings in the participant contract**. **[Law] [BA]**
- **Cost:** certifier fees for the provider and one course, auditor travel, a Berlin VAT-exemption certificate, and maybe a QM consultant. Then yearly surveillance fees. The exact amounts come from quotes; I keep them private.
- **Duration:** typically 10 to 12 weeks from the start of preparation to certificates for a new provider with help; 2 to 3 months from the application if the QM system is ready; 6 to 9 months without QM (consultant estimates **[Sec]**). There is no legal deadline for the FKS; one suspension of up to 3 months for fixes is allowed (§ 181 (4) SGB III) **[Law]**.
- **April 2027: realistic but tight.** It works if we sign with an FKS by the end of November, submit documents around Christmas, and get both certificates by mid-February. One 3-month suspension pushes the start to May or June. Critical path: QM documentation + course concept + documented costs for the surcharge → an audit slot in January.
- **Entity: apply as my existing sole proprietorship** (trading as DataTalksClub). Natural persons can be providers. A new GmbH or UG adds weeks before the FKS contract, and the Trägerzulassung is bound to the legal person, so a later switch means a new Trägerzulassung.

## 1. Trägerzulassung (provider approval)

**What is checked** (§ 178 SGB III, § 2 AZAV) **[Law]**:

1. **Capacity (Leistungsfähigkeit):** insolvency declaration; organization and staff; rooms participants use; current offers. The FKS can ask for financial evidence **[Law] [Sec]**.
2. **Reliability (Zuverlässigkeit):** personal and business data, register extract if registered, a declaration about criminal records and trade bans in the last 5 years **[Law]**.
3. **Integration (Eingliederung):** cooperation with local labour-market actors; methods to follow labour-market trends; past courses and outcomes; ratings by participants and employers. **A new provider can instead explain "in geeigneter Weise" how it will meet each point (§ 2 (7) AZAV)** **[Law]**. Our evidence: employer letters of intent from the DataTalksClub network, our job-market research, survey templates.
4. **Staff:** CVs, qualifications and experience of the management and teachers; teaching suitability; participant ratings of teachers **[Law]**. No specific teaching certificate is required **[Sec]**; DataTalksClub course history (Zoomcamps, ratings, testimonials) is the evidence **[Inf]**.
5. **QM system** (§ 2 (4) AZAV), documented in 9 points **[Law]**: mission statement; organization and management incl. goals and internal audits; staff development; target agreements with KPIs and improvement loop; labour-market developments in design and delivery; methods for individual learning and integration; evaluation of courses and their labour-market results; cooperation with third parties; complaint management incl. regular participant surveys. See the [QM handbook draft](azav-qm-handbook.md).
   - **ISO 9001 is not required.** An ISO certificate can only be taken into account **[FKS] [Sec]**.
   - The system must be **applied**, not just written **[Inf]**. We run the free sessions on 23 November, 30 November and 7 December 2026 through the QM processes (feedback survey, complaint channel, KPI log, management review) so there are records for the audit.
   - For a one-person provider we document **who takes over if I am ill** (Vertretungsregelung). The Beirat asks for it **[BA]**. Decided: named **backup teachers** on standby, each with a CV and a written agreement.
6. **Participant contract** with fair withdrawal and termination terms; a certificate of attendance with content, duration and goal at the end (§ 178 Nr. 5 SGB III, § 2 (5) AZAV) **[Law]**.
7. **Field:** apply only for Fachbereich 4, berufliche Weiterbildung (§ 5 (1) Nr. 4 AZAV) **[Law]**.

**Premises for an online provider** **[BA]**: per the Beirat recommendations (17 June 2026), the certificate lists the business seat and the "Standorte, von denen aus digitale Maßnahmen administriert werden". A location can only be added once it is actually rented. **Open question for the FKS:** is a home office fine as the administering location, or should I rent a small coworking office (which also covers hybrid Berlin days)? The on-site audit always visits the main seat **[FKS]**.

**Process** **[FKS]**: offer → signed contract → application and documents about **4 to 6 weeks before the audit** → stage 1 document review → stage 2 on-site audit → certification committee → certificate. Provider and course reviews can run **in parallel**. The FKS cannot also be our consultant (§ 177 (2) Nr. 3 SGB III) **[Sec]**.

**Validity:** the provider certificate lasts at most 5 years with annual surveillance (§ 181 (5) with § 177 (3) SGB III) **[Law]**.

## 2. Maßnahmezulassung (course approval)

- **Legal basis:** §§ 179, 180 SGB III; §§ 3, 4, 5 AZAV; Beirat recommendations **[Law]**. The course must end with a certificate of the content taught **[Sec]**.
- **What to submit** (Beirat, pp. 19–20) **[BA]**: concept (goals, content, methods, materials, teaching organization, substitution rules, tech support, attendance control); success control; how we check that entrants fit (our code screen); labour-market fit; rooms, staff and **IT equipment** for teachers and participants; data protection; occupational safety; a cost and revenue calculation (Kalkulation).
- **Calculation basis:** 12 participants (§ 3 (3) AZAV) **[Law]**. The planned group of about 15 to 20 is an assumption, not the legal basis. Up to +25% over the B-DKS the FKS decides alone; above +25% the BA must agree **[BA]**. If the documented special costs do not reach +25%, we file a lower surcharge.
- **Sampling (Referenzauswahl):** only for courses at or below the B-DKS (§ 5 (3) AZAV) **[Law]**. **A course with a surcharge is reviewed individually**, which takes longer and costs more **[FKS]**.
- **Evidence:** "Eigenerklärungen des Trägers (ohne Nachweise) genügen nicht" **[BA]**. We need real quotes or invoices for AI-tool seats, cloud/GPU budgets, Zoom and GitHub, mentor and backup-teacher contracts, and a room contract if the course is in person.
- **Online delivery:** counts as a "digitale Maßnahme" **[BA]**. Attendance must be provable (login records plus teacher checks), because the Agentur can audit attendance (§ 183 SGB III) **[Sec]**.
- **No recordings promised in the contract.** Contractual recordings count as asynchronous teaching. If asynchronous parts outweigh live parts, ZFU approval becomes mandatory (§ 3 (7) AZAV) **[Law] [BA]**. Asynchronous parts also do not count as UE **[BA]**.
- **Subcontracting:** at most 10% to non-certified subcontractors (Beirat 5.2) **[BA]**. Backup teachers and mentors work directly for the provider as freelancers **[Inf]**. The transitional rule in § 127 SGB IV protects freelance teachers from reclassification until **31 December 2027** if both sides assume self-employment and the teacher agrees **[Law]**. That covers the 2027 cohorts; plan for 2028.
- **English teaching:** allowed; the language is not a reason to reject, but the Vermittler judges the job prospects **[Sec]**.
- **Validity:** normally at most 3 years (§ 5 (4) AZAV) **[Law]**. Changes to the concept or price need a change approval **[BA] [FKS]**.

**After the certificates:**

- Enter the course in **KURSNET**, the BA provider system; the public search is called "mein NOW" since 2025. Tick "Zulassung zur Förderung mit Bildungsgutschein" and add the start date **[BA/Sec]**.
- Participants get the Bildungsgutschein from their Agentur (since 1 January 2025 also as Jobcenter clients). It is usually valid for up to 3 months, and the course must start in that window (§ 81 (4) SGB III) **[Sec]**. The provider gives each candidate a written Bildungsangebot with the course number, dates and price **[Sec]**.

## 3. Choosing a certifier (FKS)

There are about 30 accredited FKS. The binding list is the [DAkkS database](https://www.dakks.de/de/akkreditierte-stellen-suche.html) (search "Arbeitsförderung") **[BA]**.

Candidates I looked at: GUTcert (Berlin, so little auditor travel, online quote tool for small providers), APV-Zertifizierungs GmbH and QUACERT (both publish price lists), Cert-IT (focus on IT and education providers), CERTQUA (large education certifier), and TÜV Hessen as a reference.

**Plan [Inf]:** ask several FKS for quotes, with the same questions:

1. Is a home office fine as the administering location for a digital course, or do we need a rented room?
2. Can you audit in January 2027, and how long from complete documents to certificates?
3. What is your process and fee for one course at up to +25% over the B-DKS (KldB 43414, 520 UE)?
4. Do you have a Kalkulation template?
5. Is English as the teaching language a problem?
6. Can the provider and course reviews run in parallel?

## 4. Cost items

- Certifier fees for the provider (initial) and for one course above the B-DKS.
- Auditor travel (close to zero with a Berlin certifier).
- Yearly surveillance fees in years 2 to 5; re-certification after 5 years; course renewal after 3 years.
- ZFU: none if the teaching is live; otherwise a fee based on the course price.
- The Berlin VAT-exemption certificate (§ 4 Nr. 21 a bb UStG).
- Optional: a QM consultant or QM templates.
- Change approvals.
- Liability insurance: not required by § 2 AZAV, but an FKS may ask.
- My own time: several weeks for QM and the concept.

The total is small compared with one cohort, and much smaller if we build QM in-house with templates.

## 5. Timeline back from April 2027

**Benchmarks:** 4 to 10 weeks of preparation and 10 to 12 weeks in total to the certificate **[Sec]**; documents 4 to 6 weeks before the audit **[FKS]**; 2 to 3 months from the application with QM ready, 6 to 9 months without **[Sec]**; a possible 3-month suspension (§ 181 (4) SGB III) **[Law]**.

**Target start: Monday 12 April 2027** (Easter Monday is 29 March 2027).

| By | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 20 Nov 2026 | Entity decision; quote requests to several certifiers |
| 30 Nov | Certifier chosen and contract signed; audit date in mid-January reserved; QM build starts |
| 23 Nov – 7 Dec | Free sessions run through the QM processes: survey, complaints, KPI log |
| 18 Dec | QM handbook (9 points) and processes; course concept, Kalkulation with real quotes, participant contract, substitution, attendance method |
| 21 Dec | Application for provider and course submitted in parallel |
| early Jan 2027 | Stage 1 document review; fix findings |
| mid-Jan | On-site audit (stage 2) |
| **by ~12 Feb** | **Provider and course certificates** (the critical date) |
| mid-Feb | KURSNET entry; VAT certificate request; Bildungsangebote ready |
| mid-Feb – end of Mar | Candidates get vouchers, entry code screen, contracts |
| 12 Apr 2027 | Cohort 1 starts |

**Verdict [Inf]:** April is **doable with about 6 to 8 weeks of buffer**. That absorbs a normal correction loop, but not a full 3-month suspension. **Fallbacks:** (a) file the course at the B-DKS or a lower documented surcharge if the +25% evidence is not ready, and raise it later with a change approval; (b) move the start to May or June 2027. We do not market "funded by Bildungsgutschein" before both certificates exist. We say "applying".

## 6. Which entity applies

**Rules:** a provider can be a natural person, a legal person or a partnership (§ 21 SGB III; § 2 (1) AZAV) **[Law]**. Each person needs its own Trägerzulassung; group certificates are not allowed **[FKS]**.

**Decision (14 November 2026):** apply as my existing sole proprietorship.

- It is the fastest route: it exists and is registered for tax, so the FKS contract can be signed this month.
- A GmbH needs a notary, share capital, a commercial register entry and a bank account first. That pushes the FKS contract to January and makes April unlikely.
- Downsides: unlimited personal liability, and Academy revenue mixes with other DataTalksClub revenue for VAT.
- A later move to a GmbH means a new Trägerzulassung. Decide after cohort 1.
- The business registration should list training (Weiterbildung / Schulungen) before the FKS application.

**VAT (§ 4 Nr. 21 a bb UStG):**

- Since the 2024 Annual Tax Act, the exemption still needs a certificate from the state authority that the provider delivers "Ausbildung, Fortbildung oder berufliche Umschulung" ([BMF letter of 24 October 2025](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-24-ust-befreiung-schul-und-bildungszweck.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=4)) **[BA]**.
- In Berlin the likely authority is [SenASGIVA](https://www.berlin.de/sen/arbeit/weiterbildung/umsatzsteuerbefreiung/). The application needs the curriculum, teacher qualifications, business registration, sample contracts and certificates.
- Freelance teachers can then also invoice without VAT with the provider's confirmation (UStAE 4.21.3 (4)) **[BA]**.
- Why it matters **[Inf]**: if the approved price had to include 19% VAT, the net amount per seat would shrink a lot. With the exemption, input VAT on Academy costs is not deductible.
- Check with the tax adviser before filing.

## 7. Next steps

1. Confirm the entity and ask the tax adviser about the VAT exemption and separate Academy accounting.
2. Update the business registration to include training.
3. Send quote requests to several certifiers with the six questions above.
4. Choose the QM route: build it in-house from § 2 (4) AZAV and the Beirat text, or hire a consultant (who cannot be the FKS).
5. Location: home office or a small rented coworking office, depending on the FKS answers.
6. Course documents: turn the course design and the cost memo into the concept, the Kalkulation with real quotes, the participant contract (live teaching only, no recording promise), certificate templates, the attendance method and the substitution rule.
7. Backup teachers and mentors: name them and collect a CV and a written agreement for each.
8. Employer letters of intent: 3 to 5 Berlin companies from the DataTalksClub network that would give project briefs, come to demo days or consider graduates. Evidence for § 2 (2) AZAV.
9. Free sessions as QM evidence: feedback survey, teacher rating and complaint channel; keep the records.
10. VAT certificate: request it after the course concept exists (January to February).
