# Nemusli Consult and the Closed Jobcenter Coaching Program

Research from 15 November 2026. This is an AVGS coaching case, not a Bildungsgutschein Weiterbildung competitor. A small new Träger received AZAV certification, ran a voucher program for about eight months, then closed it and handed the licence back.

## 1. In short

- The instrument was AVGS (Aktivierungs- und Vermittlungsgutschein, § 45 SGB III), not a Bildungsgutschein for Umschulung or Weiterbildung. The offer was online one-to-one Jobcoaching (75 Unterrichtseinheiten) and Existenzgründungscoaching (110 UE). The sales page sometimes mixes the names "Bildungsgutschein/AVGS".
- The Träger was Nemusli GmbH in Bamberg (Amtsgericht Bamberg HRB 12020, registered 31 March 2025), not a partner school. The site names EUROPANOZERT as the certifier.
- They announced accreditation between June and September 2025, with a start in October 2025. On 29 June 2026 the founder wrote that she already stopped working with the state in May 2026, handed the licence back and closed the project. Existing clients would finish; new applications were closed.
- The founder named these reasons: invoices went unpaid because the GmbH was not exempt from VAT; Jobcenters prefer offline providers "around the corner"; and it was hard to find enough strong Russian- and Ukrainian-speaking coaches.
- On 15 November 2026 the program page was still online and still invited applications. That page no longer describes a current offer.

## 3. Why it closed

The founder's own announcement, not a statement from the Agentur or the certifier.

From [t.me/nemusli/6398](https://t.me/nemusli/6398) (29 June 2026, 09:37 Europe/Berlin):

- She refused to keep working with the state, handed the licence back and closed the coaching project for Jobcenter and Agentur für Arbeit employment and business coachings.
- She already did this in May, and stayed silent until this post.
- The documentation firm, she says, was contractually supposed to prepare them for operations and never warned them about important details.
- It turned out the GmbH needed to be exempt from VAT, and that step was missing. Because the company was not exempt, invoices for completed work went unpaid.
- Online coachings, she says, are not really liked at the Jobcenter.
- Existing clients would finish; new applications were no longer accepted.
- She frames it as both a personal failure and an honest step, and says she is building her own paid products instead, including for people on benefits.

From [t.me/nemusli/6412](https://t.me/nemusli/6412) (1 July 2026, 12:31 Europe/Berlin), two problems that she says finally finished her:

- Staff: few strong Russian- and Ukrainian-speaking coaches who care about results; screening a hundred applications to hire three or four people; that admin time is unpaid because it is already built into the coaching rate. Firms that hire anyone, she says, make money; her quality bar did not.
- Steering: in Germany, Jobcenters are forbidden by law from recommending or sending people to specific companies. In practice, she says, this happens all the time. A client arrives with her letter and program and is told to take a voucher only to "the firm around the corner." She says this happened too often to be a coincidence.
- She also repeats stories of low-quality funded coaching, such as a Spree outing instead of interview prep, or a list of vacancies and a request to write cover letters.
- She says she cannot and will not work at a loss with a broken system, and she no longer wants anything to do with that system.

Earlier posts already describe the same steering problem, before the closure: [t.me/nemusli/5630](https://t.me/nemusli/5630) (16 October 2025) and [t.me/nemusli/5727](https://t.me/nemusli/5727) (15 November 2025, first vouchers after a tour of Jobcenters and a mailing to 400+ offices).

A Bildungsträger needs a § 4 Nr. 21 UStG certificate so the Agentur can pay tax-free invoices. This is on the [AZAV certification plan](azav-certification.md).

The program page [nemusli-consult.com/jobcenter](https://nemusli-consult.com/jobcenter) was still live and still inviting applications on 15 November 2026, four and a half months after the closure post. Treat it as a stale page, not as proof that the program is open.

## 4. What this means for DataTalksClub Academy

This is AVGS coaching, not our Bildungsgutschein Weiterbildung product. The measure type and audience differ. The operational lessons still apply.

1. **AZAV is not the same as vouchers.** They were certified and listed, according to the founder, and still needed to introduce themselves in person to Jobcenters. Berater steered people to local offline providers they already knew. An English online program will meet the same "we don't know you" and "we prefer a school around the corner" pattern. Certification and a mein NOW line are not enough. I need a Berater pack and a way to be a known option in the offices that will actually issue vouchers. This sits next to the [Berater objections guide](berater-objections.md).

2. **The Träger's tax and billing setup can block payment even after a course is delivered.** They missed the VAT exemption and say invoices were refused. The § 4 Nr. 21 UStG certificate and the billing entity have to be right before the first cohort, not after the first invoice. Online delivery also needs to be explicit in the approved Maßnahme, because Berater can treat "online" as a reason to send the person elsewhere.
