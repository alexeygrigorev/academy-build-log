## How the Score Works

An earlier ranking put "AI Engineering vs Agentic Engineering" first because "ai engineering" has 3,600 searches a month in Germany. That volume will not fill the waitlist. The new score counts buyer intent and conversion fit twice, and volume once.

Total = Intent x 2 + Fit x 2 + Volume + Feasibility + AEO. Maximum 35. Volume, difficulty and the live top results come from Serpstat's Germany database and from live Google Germany checks on 15 November 2026. "Unknown" means Serpstat had no row. No number is estimated.

- Intent: 5 if the reader is comparing voucher-eligible programs or preparing an appointment; 1 if the piece only defines a term.
- Volume: 5 if 5,000 or more monthly searches; 1 if unknown. If the main query is German and the article is English, volume drops one level.
- Feasibility: 5 if datatalks.club's domain authority (34) beats the current top 3, or if the top 3 has no matching page; 1 if a much stronger domain owns a matching page.
- AEO: 5 if the query is a question we can answer in two sentences and current AI answers are generic; 1 if official government pages already own the answer.
- Fit: 5 if the reader is an experienced developer in Germany who could hold a voucher; 1 if the audience is global or beginner.

Tier: bottom-funnel Academy first, then mid-funnel Academy, then site-wide pieces that mainly help the rest of the site and the Zoomcamps. Those last three are kept. They are not expected to move Academy waitlist signups much.

## Two Phases

Until an AZAV certificate exists, voucher content stays careful.

Phase 1 (now): only low-key pages that can already be indexed, so Google and AI tools can learn that DataTalksClub Academy is an English AI program designed for Bildungsgutschein funding, coming soon. The single German page `/academy/bildungsgutschein/` goes live, plus two informational guides: "Bildungsgutschein in English" (17 November) and "Laid Off as a Developer in Germany: What to Do in the First Four Weeks" (8 December), which explains registration and Arbeitslosengeld and mentions training in one short section. Other Phase 1 articles (bootcamp comparisons, format, Zoomcamps or the Academy, and the three site-wide pieces) may include the funding line once, in the facts block. They are not voucher campaigns. There is no ad, newsletter or outreach push for the voucher story in Phase 1.

Phase 2 (after the certificate exists): run the swap checklist below, add the KURSNET listing link and the Maßnahmenummer, then publish the voucher-centric articles in this order: the appointment guide, the B2 German guide, the voucher catalogue, what to do if the voucher is refused, the KURSNET guide, the cost guide, then Arbeitslosengeld during training. Only after the first of those are live comes any push. No certificate date is assumed. January and February slots are held for this list and stay empty if there is no certificate.

URLs, slugs and titles are stable from the first day, so the text can be swapped later without changing the address.
