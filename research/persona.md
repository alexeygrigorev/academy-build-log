# Persona: Who the Academy Is For

Draft, 14 November 2026. Based on [laid-off developers and the Agentur](laid-off-developers-and-agentur.md), [market sizing](market-sizing.md) and the audience research for the free sessions. Original language: Russian. This is the English version.

**Who.** A developer, 30 to 45 years old, with 5 to 15 years of experience (backend, full-stack, data). Lives in Berlin, often an expat: English at work, everyday German. Worked at a startup or scale-up that cut staff.

**Situation.** Laid off, or working through the notice period (Kündigungsfrist). Registered with the Agentur to get ALG I, but does not think about courses. Job hunting for months: many applications, few answers. Job ads ask more and more for AI, agents, LLMs, Claude Code.

**What they think.**

- My experience is losing value. The market moved to AI.
- I tried agents, but I never really used them at work. I have nothing to show on my CV.
- Voucher courses are for beginners and career changers.
- I can't lose half a year on a course.
- The Vermittler will say I am employable anyway.

**What they want.** In 2 to 3 months: the skills from the job ads and a project to show in interviews. Taught at an experienced level, by practitioners. Free through a Bildungsgutschein while ALG I runs (§ 148 SGB III: two days of course use up one day of ALG I).

**Where to find them.** LinkedIn (#opentowork, posts about layoffs), Berlin meetups, Slack and Telegram chats for expats and developers, r/berlin, r/cscareerquestionsEU, Berlin job newsletters, Russian-speaking data science and developer chats.

**Why us.** Not from zero and not 2019-era ML: we teach experienced developers to ship a product with coding agents and own the result. Short, live, in English, from a known name with large free courses.

**Secondary audience.** Employed developers who fear falling behind, if their employer pays (§ 82 SGB III), maybe part-time.

**Not our audience.** Beginners, Quereinsteiger, people who want to "get into IT".

## Interviews (14 and 15 November 2026)

I posted a call for 20 to 30 minute interviews in a Russian-speaking data science community to check this persona and the format before AZAV. Notes are in [interviews.md](interviews.md).

Additions, without rewriting the persona above:

- There is an adjacent test engineer segment interested in the AI Engineering track.
- In at least one Berlin case, the counsellor offers a Bildungsgutschein only after a B2 German exam.
- People moving from academic ML into industry are another adjacent segment.
