# Facebook Groups for the Hireable With AI Sessions

Desk research, 15 November 2026. I looked for Facebook groups where I can tell experienced software developers in Germany about three free English-language online sessions, Hireable With AI, on Monday 23 November, 30 November and 7 December 2026, at 17:00 Europe/Berlin. The registration page is [datatalks.club/academy/sessions/](https://datatalks.club/academy/sessions/). I did not join any group and I did not post.

The people I want to reach have two or more years of experience, live in Germany (many in Berlin), and are job hunting, laid off, in a notice period, or upskilling. Many are expats on a Blue Card. The Bildungsgutschein is one way some of them can later pay for a course.

I used web search and public pages that list Facebook group URLs. I only put a group in the main list when I found Google-indexed posts on that exact group path, or a 2025–2026 public page that printed the exact URL. Facebook itself showed a login page, so I could not read member counts, privacy settings or group rules on Facebook. I only wrote a member count when another site published one, and I kept the source and the date. If I did not find a published count, I left it blank. I did not invent groups or numbers.

I did not find a verified English-speaking Facebook group about Arbeitslosigkeit or the Bildungsgutschein. InterNations Berlin is a separate network, not a Facebook group.

## Other groups I found

I also found groups that I did not put in the main list, because the fit was weaker or I only had an older source.

Social or classifieds groups with recent indexed posts included International Friends in Berlin (https://www.facebook.com/groups/friendsinberlin), German Freelance Visa Help (https://www.facebook.com/groups/germanfreelancevisa), PAKISTANI IN BERLIN (https://www.facebook.com/groups/299793255001114), and two Russian Berlin advice or classifieds groups (https://www.facebook.com/groups/495520187299661 and https://www.facebook.com/groups/1691327001150700). General German job boards included https://www.facebook.com/groups/berlin.stellenmarkt and https://www.facebook.com/groups/382245705239865. A Filipinos in Germany group (https://www.facebook.com/groups/FilipinoCommunityinGermany) had Ausbildung posts, not experienced-developer jobs. Study in Germany (https://www.facebook.com/groups/studygermany) is for students.

A 14 April 2021 [Berlin Facebook guide](https://www.natalieetc.com/home/facebook-groups-to-join-if-you-live-in-berlin) also listed English Activities (Berlin, Germany) (https://www.facebook.com/groups/English.activities.Berlin/), English Speaking Jobs in Berlin (https://www.facebook.com/groups/jobinberlin), English Speaking Jobs in Berlin, Germany (https://www.facebook.com/groups/957238694427799/), Find A Job in Berlin (https://www.facebook.com/groups/findajobinberlinnow/), Berlin Freelancers (https://www.facebook.com/groups/berlinfreelancers), Berlin 4 Beginners (https://www.facebook.com/groups/Berlin4Beginners), and another Blue Card group (https://www.facebook.com/groups/136525830377363/). I did not confirm that those URLs are still active.

A [2020 study of Brazilians in Germany](https://mpeinternacional.uff.br/wp-content/uploads/sites/53/2020/04/Relat%C3%B3rio-de-pesquisa-Alemanha_VFinal.pdf) listed Brasileiros e Brasileiras em Berlim (https://www.facebook.com/groups/brasileiros.brasileiras.berlim/), Brasileiros Na Alemanha (https://www.facebook.com/groups/Brasilerada/), and Trabalho / Emprego na Alemanha (https://www.facebook.com/groups/1821152048096815/). The member counts in that paper are from 2020 and are not current. The study also said many of those groups were closed. A 2020 academic article on Turkish migration in Berlin listed New Wave in Berlin (https://www.facebook.com/groups/newwaveinberlin/), Ötekilerin Berlin Dalgası (https://www.facebook.com/groups/235696503857616/) and New Wave in Germany (https://www.facebook.com/groups/newwaveingermany/). I did not find recent indexed posts for those URLs.
