# Academy Research

This folder has the research I did for the DataTalks.Club Academy: a full-time AI program for experienced developers in Berlin, planned for April 2027. These are cleaned-up copies of my working notes. I removed names of interviewees and respondents, private messages, prices from partners and vendors, budgets, and internal tools and links.

Most notes are from 13 to 15 November 2026. New research goes here as it happens.

## Audience

- [persona.md](persona.md): who the academy is for.
- [audience-and-messaging.md](audience-and-messaging.md): audience segments, message angles and the "Hireable With AI" series name.
- [laid-off-developers-and-agentur.md](laid-off-developers-and-agentur.md): laid-off developers in Berlin, the Agentur für Arbeit and how many people we can reach.
- [market-sizing.md](market-sizing.md): market size, audience and signals for the two tracks.
- [german-vs-skills.md](german-vs-skills.md): how much German matters compared to skills in Berlin tech.

- [interviews.md](interviews.md): four discovery interviews, anonymized (roles only).
