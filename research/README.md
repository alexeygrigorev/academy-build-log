# Academy Research

This folder has the research I did for the DataTalks.Club Academy: a full-time AI program for experienced developers in Berlin, planned for April 2027.

Most notes are from 13 to 15 November 2026. New research goes here as it happens.

## Audience

- [persona.md](persona.md): who the academy is for.
- [audience-and-messaging.md](audience-and-messaging.md): audience segments, message angles and the "Hireable With AI" series name.
- [laid-off-developers-and-agentur.md](laid-off-developers-and-agentur.md): laid-off developers in Berlin, the Agentur für Arbeit and how many people we can reach.
- [market-sizing.md](market-sizing.md): market size, audience and signals for the two tracks.
- [german-vs-skills.md](german-vs-skills.md): how much German matters compared to skills in Berlin tech.
- [b2-german-rule.md](b2-german-rule.md): the B2 German rule for Bildungsgutschein courses, the exceptions, the Munich case, and how many people in Berlin this leaves.

## Interviews and Community Signals

- [interviews.md](interviews.md): four discovery interviews, anonymized (roles only).
- [reddit-discovery.md](reddit-discovery.md): the public Reddit posts I used to find interviewees, and why those subreddits.
- [voucher-course-complaints.md](voucher-course-complaints.md): public Reddit quotes on why experienced developers dislike voucher courses.
- [berater-objections.md](berater-objections.md): why Berater refuse a Bildungsgutschein and how to talk about it.
- [testimonials.md](testimonials.md): public quotes from people who took my courses (Maven reviews, blog testimonials, LinkedIn posts).
