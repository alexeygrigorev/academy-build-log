# DataTalksClub Academy Marketing Plan, November 2026 to April 2027

Date: 15 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin). This is my marketing plan for the first cohort of the Academy. Related pages: [article plan](article-plan.md), [competitor keyword research](competitor-keywords.md), [content audit](content-audit.md).

On the site the brand is written DataTalks.Club Academy. On social and in this build log it is DataTalksClub Academy.

How to read the numbers: each figure is either cited to a research page of this build log or to a web link, or it is labeled "target" or "assumption". Targets are what I aim for. They are not forecasts.

## 1. The Offer in One Paragraph

DataTalksClub Academy is a full-time live program in English for experienced developers in Germany (2+ years of experience). There are two programs. Agentic Engineering is based on AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp and my experience running coding agents 24/7 for the last year. AI Engineering is based on LLM Zoomcamp, AI Engineering Buildcamp and my experience building AI products and putting them in production. Each program runs 3 months (13 weeks), Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:30 Berlin time, in Berlin or live online, with 15 seats. Start: April 2027. The programs are designed for Bildungsgutschein funding (AZAV). I teach them myself, and I have taught more than 100,000 students in DataTalksClub courses.

The primary CTA on every academy page and every article is "Join the Waitlist". The primary KPI everywhere is confirmed voucher-eligible waitlist signups.

The free "Hireable With AI" Zoom sessions (Mon 23 Nov, Mon 30 Nov, Mon 7 Dec 2026, 17:00 Berlin time) are separate promo events. They never appear inside the course schedule, and the course schedule never appears as session dates.

## 2. Goal and Funnel Math

### 2.1 Goal

15 people start in April 2027. The primary KPI before April is confirmed (double opt-in) waitlist signups from voucher-eligible people, not raw signups.

### 2.2 What "Voucher-Eligible" Means

A waitlist signup counts as voucher-eligible when all five are true: lives in Germany; 2+ years as a developer; unemployed, in a notice period or registered as job seeking; can study full days (Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:30) for 3 months; German at B2 or better, or B1 with a German plan. The last point comes from the [B2 German rule research](b2-german-rule.md), section 7.1.

Not my audience: beginners and career changers (the free Zoomcamps are for them), people who want a German-language course (the German page says so and points them elsewhere), and people with German at A2 or below as voucher candidates. That last group is welcome in the free sessions.

### 3.2 Positioning

Positioning: free courses teach from zero or teach 2019-era ML; I teach experienced developers to ship products with coding agents and own the result. Public copy names the developer baseline and the work taught.

Public proof points, all from my research:

- In a week of postings (7 to 14 Nov 2026), 28 of 154 developer job ads in Berlin (18.2%) and 86 of 588 in Germany (14.6%) named Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Codex or AI-assisted coding ([supply, jobs and bootcamps research](supply-jobs-and-bootcamps.md), section 1).
- Among voucher-fundable courses in the BA catalogue, 3,071 mention prompt engineering, 8 mention LangGraph and 4 mention evals (same page, section 3.1).
- KURSNET listed no 3 to 4 month, English, live program for experienced developers with coding agents as the main subject on 14 Nov 2026 (same page, section 4.2). The closest is Digitale Leute "AI Software Engineering", which also admits beginners.
- Six months after a BA-funded IT course, 38.7% are in insured employment in Germany and 35.6% in Berlin ([market sizing research](market-sizing.md), section 3.2). I do not promise placement. I answer the adviser's question with a shipped project, interview preparation in weeks 10 to 13, and current job ads.

What I do not claim: that all bootcamps are outdated. The supply research confirmed only the narrow version (same page, section 3.3). GFN and alfatraining already teach Claude Code and MCP in 2 to 4 week German modules, and Turing College teaches a current LLM stack.
