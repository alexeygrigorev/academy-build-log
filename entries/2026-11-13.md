## The Backstory

Early in 2026, I launched [AI Engineering Buildcamp on Maven](https://maven.com/alexey-grigorev/from-rag-to-agents). People asked if they could use a Bildungsgutschein to pay for it through the Arbeitsagentur. This is a [training voucher](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein). I looked into offering courses funded this way. I wasn't ready to commit that much time and money.

In early November, I used [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) to research the topic again. A launch in 2027 looked possible.

By late 2026, I automated much of my work and could fund AZAV certification. This gave me time and money to work on the academy. I decided to go ahead.

## Friday's Survey

On Friday, 13 November, I shared [Survey: intensive AI and software development bootcamp](https://forms.gle/XUc29pDjsrLJwFaS7) through my personal channels. I also shared it in [my X post at 10:00 Berlin time](https://x.com/Al_Grigor/status/2121166242975551861). I wanted to learn which track people wanted: [Agentic Engineering](https://academy.datatalks.club/agentic-engineering/) or [AI Engineering](https://academy.datatalks.club/ai-engineering/). I asked about studying online or in Berlin. I described an April 2027 start, about 15 places and a plan to accept Bildungsgutschein funding.

By about 10:48 on 14 November, the X post showed 761 impressions, six likes, three replies and two bookmarks. It got no reposts. This told me about reach and reactions, rather than preference for a track.

I needed to reach more experienced developers in Germany and make it easier to show interest. The first two course pages were live at [DataTalksClub Academy](https://academy.datatalks.club/). Each track had a separate shortlist. I plan to compare interest in the two programs through those lists.

## The First Demand Test

The first site went live through [PR #1](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/academy/pull/1), followed by [review fixes in PR #2](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/academy/pull/2). Both tracks had an email-required shortlist form. I wanted to send people to each page and compare interest before I chose the first cohort.

I started with about 15 places and an April 2027 target. I also considered a 20-person first cohort while planning my course promotion. I used about 15 places in the academy demand test. AZAV certification and voucher funding are plans.

I also planned a December campaign for my January [Maven course](https://maven.com/alexey-grigorev/from-rag-to-agents), with one talk each week. I kept that promotion on my personal accounts. For the academy, I used the approach from [Oversubscribed](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/oversubscribed-how-to-get-people-lining-up-to-do-business-with-you-2nd-edition-p-9780857088253): learn what people want before opening places.

The [audience research](https://github.com/alexeygrigorev/academy-build-log/blob/main/research/audience-and-messaging.md) explains why I later widened the outreach beyond my own accounts.
