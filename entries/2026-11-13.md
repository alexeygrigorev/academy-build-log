## The Backstory

Early in 2026, I launched [AI Engineering Buildcamp on Maven](https://maven.com/alexey-grigorev/from-rag-to-agents). People asked if they could use a Bildungsgutschein to pay for it through the Arbeitsagentur. This is a [training voucher](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein). I looked into offering courses funded this way. I was not ready to commit that much time and money.

In early November, I used [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) to research the topic again. A launch in 2027 looked possible.

By late 2026, I automated much of my work and could fund AZAV certification. This gave me time and money to work on the academy. I decided to go ahead.

## Friday's Survey

On Friday, 13 November, I shared [Survey: intensive AI and software development bootcamp](https://forms.gle/XUc29pDjsrLJwFaS7) through my personal channels. I also shared it in [my X post at 10:00 Berlin time](https://x.com/Al_Grigor/status/2121166242975551861). I wanted to learn which track people wanted: [Agentic Engineering](https://academy.datatalks.club/agentic-engineering/) or [AI Engineering](https://academy.datatalks.club/ai-engineering/). I asked about studying online or in Berlin. I described an April 2027 start, about 15 places and a plan to accept Bildungsgutschein funding.

By Saturday morning, I reported four early responses through my channels. I could not yet count the answers in the form. I did not have a reliable total or a breakdown by track. These early responses were not enough to choose a program.

I needed to reach more experienced developers in Germany and make it easier to show interest. The first two course pages were live at [DataTalksClub Academy](https://academy.datatalks.club/). Each track had a separate shortlist. I plan to compare interest in the two programs through those lists.
