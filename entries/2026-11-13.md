## The Backstory

Early in 2026, I launched a paid course. People started asking whether they could pay for it through the Arbeitsagentur with a Bildungsgutschein, a training voucher. I looked into offering courses funded that way. It would take a lot of money and time, and I wasn't ready.

In early November, I started researching the topic again in ChatGPT. I realised that doing this in 2027 was quite realistic.

By late 2026, I'd automated a lot of my work and had the funds for AZAV accreditation. That gave me the time and money to work on it, so I decided to go ahead.

## Friday's Survey

On Friday, 13 November, I shared a Google Forms survey through my personal channels. I wanted to know which of two tracks people wanted: Agentic Engineering or AI Engineering. The survey also asked about studying online or in person in Berlin. I described a course starting in April 2027, with about 15 places, and the plan to accept Bildungsgutschein funding.

By Saturday morning, I'd reported four early responses through my channels. We couldn't yet count the answers in the form, so I didn't have a reliable total or a breakdown by track. I couldn't use those early responses to choose a program.

I needed to reach more of the intended audience and make it easier for people to express interest. The first two course landing pages were already up at [academy.datatalks.club](https://academy.datatalks.club/), with a separate shortlist for each track. We'd use those shortlists to compare interest in the two programs.
