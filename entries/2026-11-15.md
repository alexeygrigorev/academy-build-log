## Staying on DataTalksClub

I needed to choose where the academy should live.

The inputs were search visibility and the fact that DataTalksClub already has years of public courses and inbound links on [datatalks.club](https://datatalks.club/).

The options were a new, separate domain for the school, or keeping the academy on datatalks.club.

I decided to stay on datatalks.club. A new domain means building that presence again.

I asked to move the academy from its subdomain to the main site's academy path. I planned permanent redirects so the session links continue to work. I also asked for links from the main site, matching canonical URLs and an updated sitemap.

## Measuring Whether People Find Us

I prepared a search content plan around questions about AI courses, experienced developers, Berlin and Bildungsgutschein funding. I planned pages for both courses, the free sessions, common questions and a voucher guide in English and German.

I tried these questions in [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/), [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) and [Google AI Overviews](https://www.google.com/search). None named the academy in this first check. I plan to repeat the same questions each week as I publish more pages.

I asked for [Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about) checks, a sitemap submission and [Umami](https://umami.is/) analytics. I want to track search impressions, visits by source and confirmed waitlist signups for each program.
