## Staying on DataTalks.Club

I initially considered a separate domain for the school. After looking at search visibility, we decided to keep the academy on datatalks.club. We already have years of public courses and links there, and a new domain would mean building that presence again.

We then decided to move the academy from its subdomain to `datatalks.club/academy/` immediately. We planned permanent redirects from the old URLs, so links already shared for the sessions continue to work. We also requested links from the main site, matching canonical URLs and an updated sitemap. The move was in progress, with a preview and redirect checks still to come.

## Two Course Pages and Waitlists

I asked for both programs to have full course pages, even while there's no active cohort. People should be able to read about [Agentic Engineering](https://academy.datatalks.club/agentic-engineering/) or [AI Engineering](https://academy.datatalks.club/ai-engineering/) and join the relevant waitlist. We'll use the same short confirmation email for both waitlists.

We started preparing fuller course descriptions and collecting [public feedback about the earlier courses on Maven](https://maven.com/alexey-grigorev) for the pages. I want visitors to understand the programs and the teaching behind them before deciding whether to join.

The waitlist pages were merged in [public PR #18](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/academy/pull/18). The public background materials include [AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/ai-dev-tools-zoomcamp), [LLM Zoomcamp](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/llm-zoomcamp) and my writing at [AI Shipping Blog](https://aishippingblog.com/).

## Measuring Whether People Find Us

We prepared a search content plan around the questions people actually ask about AI courses, experienced developers, Berlin and Bildungsgutschein funding. We planned pages for both courses, the free sessions, a short FAQ and a voucher guide in English and German.

When we first asked ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews the questions, none named the academy. We'll repeat the same questions each week to see whether that changes as we publish the pages.

We requested Search Console checks, a sitemap submission and Umami analytics. We'll look at search impressions, visits by source and confirmed waitlist signups for each program.
