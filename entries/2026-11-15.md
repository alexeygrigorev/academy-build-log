## One Confirmation Email

This morning, the email-confirmation code was merged but still switched off while we reviewed the text. I asked for one short email after signup, with a confirmation link. The first draft included session details and other information that people didn't need in a confirmation email.

We agreed to send academy emails from the existing address for course emails. I asked for the shorter template to be enabled and checked with a real signup. That check was still in progress.

## Finishing the Session Pages

The three Meetup events now have covers using the actual DataTalks.Club logo. We replaced the drawn version so the events use the same logo as the rest of the community.

We also checked the academy pages after the copy cleanup. Some details still contradicted each other, including the duration and location. I asked for those to be made consistent with the course plan. I also asked for five different visual designs to review, keeping the approved wording.

## Staying on DataTalks.Club

I initially considered a separate domain for the school. After looking at search visibility, we decided to keep the academy on datatalks.club. We already have years of public courses and links there, and a new domain would mean building that presence again.

We then decided to move the academy from its subdomain to `datatalks.club/academy/` immediately. We planned permanent redirects from the old URLs, so links already shared for the sessions continue to work. We also requested links from the main site, matching canonical URLs and an updated sitemap. The move was in progress, with a preview and redirect checks still to come.

## Two Course Pages and Waitlists

I asked for both programs to have full course pages, even while there's no active cohort. People should be able to read about Agentic Engineering or AI Engineering and join the relevant waitlist. We'll use the same short confirmation email for both waitlists.

We started preparing fuller course descriptions and collecting public feedback about the earlier courses for the pages. I want visitors to understand the programs and the teaching behind them before deciding whether to join.

## Measuring Whether People Find Us

We prepared a search content plan around the questions people actually ask about AI courses, experienced developers, Berlin and Bildungsgutschein funding. We planned pages for both courses, the free sessions, a short FAQ and a voucher guide in English and German.

When we first asked ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews the questions, none named the academy. We'll repeat the same questions each week to see whether that changes as we publish the pages.

We requested Search Console checks, a sitemap submission and Umami analytics. We'll look at search impressions, visits by source and confirmed waitlist signups for each program.
