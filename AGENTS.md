# Build Log Writing Rules

- Keep the newest entries first. Use one page per day, Title Case titles, and public social preview metadata.
- Write as Alexey in the first person. Use simple English, short sentences and plain words. Use past simple or present only. Never use past perfect, including "had been" or "had done".
- Make each entry rich enough to reuse as a Substack post: explain why, include anonymized feedback and supported numbers, and describe what I tried and what happened. Do not invent results.
- Give every mentioned public artifact and named course a real public link. Verify links before publishing. Every entry needs relevant screenshots and all available charts. Include alt text and short captions. State the capture date if it differs from the entry date.
- Protect privacy: exclude other people's names, faces, avatars, phone numbers and emails. Never publish interview-chat screenshots or identifying survey answers. Aggregate charts are fine. Alexey's own name and avatar are fine.
- Exclude prices, budgets, internal URLs, tokens, private identifiers and machine names from text and images. Inspect every image yourself. Crop or blur sensitive content; skip anything still in doubt.
- Use clean headless browser captures without reading or copying browser profiles or cookies. Keep source captures outside this repo. Save reviewed WebPs at about quality 80, at most 1400 pixels wide, under `assets/images/YYYY-MM-DD/`.
- Write DataTalksClub without a period. Describe the academy as a full-time live program for experienced developers in Germany, with Agentic Engineering and AI Engineering tracks and an April 2027 first-cohort target. Describe AZAV certification and voucher funding as plans until confirmed. Never promise job placement.
- Commit directly to main. Keep existing public links, images and charts when updating entries. Verify the Pages build and live pages after pushing.
- Every decision gets context: inputs, options, reasoning, outcome. Show the inputs (data, numbers, what people said, constraints), the options considered, why this one, who decided, and what happened next.
- Every academy work session writes its sanitized results into this repo: `research/` for research, and `sessions/` for other session results. Commit straight to main.
- The site index intro and the bottom of every entry (from the shared layout) must say: "This log is written by AI agents from my working chats and notes. I don't edit the text." Do not put that sentence in the README.
