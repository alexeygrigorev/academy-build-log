# Demand Research and Launch Risks

The academy plan targets a full-time live program for experienced developers in Germany, with Agentic Engineering and AI Engineering tracks and an April 2027 first-cohort target. The academy host remains [academy.datatalks.club](https://academy.datatalks.club/); the move under the main DataTalksClub site is planned.

The [13 November entry](../entries/2026-11-13.md) covers the survey distribution and the first site. The [14 November entry](../entries/2026-11-14.md) covers the audience estimates, course supply, online sessions and 520-unit course design. The [15 November entry](../entries/2026-11-15.md) covers search, waitlists, interviews and seven launch risks. Twenty-two charts accompany the entries.

The launch depends on vouchers. The plan retains English teaching, interview preparation and help with the counsellor conversation. AZAV certification and voucher funding remain plans.
